# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Freshers, entry-level developers, and experienced tech professionals seeking high-match engineering roles, resume ATS optimization, and startup funding radar intelligence.

## Product Purpose
Neera AI is an autonomous, end-to-end career intelligence and job discovery platform that unifies live ATS semantic matching, resume auditing, startup funding radar, and timed daily alerts across Web and Telegram.

## Positioning
Two-tier semantic ATS matcher (30ms vector cosine similarity + recruiter-grade deep reasoning) combined with real-time startup funding radar and timed batch delivery windows.

## Operating Context
Web SPA dashboard and Telegram companion bot for timed evening digests (6–8 PM) and on-the-go alerts.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Two-Tier Resume-to-JD ATS semantic evaluation
- Live startup funding radar tracking Seed, Series A, Series B rounds
- Google-XYZ formula bullet optimizer
- Multi-channel synchronized preferences (Web + Telegram)

## Brand Commitments
Neera AI — Nexus Interface aesthetic: Warm Stone canvas (`#E4E2DD`), dark espresso surface cards (`#2C1D11`), and vibrant fiery amber/orange accents (`#F97316`).

## Product Principles
1. Mathematical precision over vague advice.
2. Signal over noise in job discovery.
3. Timed focus windows rather than perpetual interruptions.
4. Tactile, premium craft in every interaction.
