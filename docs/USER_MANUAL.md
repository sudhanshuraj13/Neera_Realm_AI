# User Manual — Neera Realm AI

## Welcome to Neera AI
Neera AI is your personal AI career, financial, and productivity assistant available 24/7 on Telegram.

---

## 1. Resume Upload & Profile Setup (`/resume`)
1. Open the bot on Telegram and type `/resume` (or tap **📄 Upload Resume**).
2. Attach your resume as a **PDF file**.
3. **Step 2/2 Experience Setup**: Telegram will display an inline keyboard:
   - `[ 🎓 Fresher (0-1 yrs) ]`
   - `[ 💻 Junior (1-3 yrs) ]`
   - `[ 🚀 Senior (3+ yrs) ]`
4. Tap your experience level to complete onboarding!

---

## 2. Searching Job Openings (`/jobs`)
1. Type `/jobs` (or tap **💼 View Matching Jobs**).
2. Neera AI scans live job listings across target companies (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) and global startup boards (Remotive, Arbeitnow).
3. Openings are filtered based on your saved experience level, target roles, and location preference.
4. Each job listing includes a direct **Apply Now** link!

---

## 3. Setting Target Dream Companies (`/target_companies`)
1. Type `/target_companies Razorpay, Stripe, OpenAI` to add target companies to your watchlist.
2. Or tell Neera AI in plain text: *"Add Google and Uber to my target companies"*.
3. Matched listings at your dream companies will display a `🎯 Dream Company` badge!

---

## 4. Daily Briefing & Watchlist (`/briefing`, `/settings`)
- Type `/briefing` to receive your instant financial & stock watchlist digest.
- Type `/settings` to add or remove stock tickers (e.g. `AAPL`, `NVDA`, `TSLA`).

---

## 5. Daily Agenda Sync (`/agenda`, `/login`)
- Type `/login` to authorize your Google Calendar.
- Type `/agenda` to view today's schedule and meeting preparation summaries.
